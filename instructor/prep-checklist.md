# Prep Checklist

Status: draft

## 6+ weeks out: approvals (these are the long poles)

- [ ] **Personal laptops allowed in the training room**, with internet access
      (guest Wi-Fi or similar). The charter requires learners' own laptops and
      free ChatGPT. At a secure facility this may need approval, or a room
      outside the controlled area. **If it can't be done, the course can't run
      as chartered.** Settle it first
- [ ] **The at-work approved AI tool confirmed**, with its authorization level,
      confirmed with the command AI or cybersecurity office. Update the
      "at work" half of [the data-rules card](../handouts/data-rules-card.md)
      and the Lab 01 key
- [ ] **Using personal ChatGPT accounts in class** cleared with that same
      office, given that every prompt uses fabricated material only
- [ ] **Certificate standing confirmed** with the training office: who signs,
      where it's recorded, and whether it carries any credit
      ([course/syllabus.md](../course/syllabus.md))
- [ ] **Course materials reviewed** under the command's process for training
      content. All examples are fabricated, but confirm none read as real
- [ ] Room booked: projector, power at every seat
- [ ] Two or three loaner laptops arranged, for anyone who can't bring one

## 2 weeks out

- [ ] Every lab **run end to end on a free ChatGPT account** by someone other than the instructor. Note anything that behaves differently from the answer keys, and any free-tier limits hit during a full day of use
- [ ] Pre-course email (student syllabus attached) and survey sent ([participants/](../participants/), [survey](../feedback/pre-course-survey.md))

## 1 week out

- [ ] Survey read: chase everyone who answered "not yet" or "can't bring a laptop" on question 3
- [ ] Slides finished ([slides/](../slides/))
- [ ] Full dry run against the clock. Capture the outputs, as a fallback if ChatGPT or the Wi-Fi is down
- [ ] Reminder email sent

## Day before

- [ ] Print: the student syllabus ([print/student-syllabus.pdf](../handouts/print/student-syllabus.pdf)), participant lab sheets **with the instructor sections removed**, the four cards, decision-memo packets, exit tickets, two sign-in sheets, index cards ([handouts/](../handouts/))
- [ ] Wall poster: the data rule
- [ ] Slides on the laptop and on removable media, if allowed

## Morning of (arrive by 7:15)

- [ ] Projector working; ChatGPT opens on the instructor machine
- [ ] Wi-Fi details on the board
- [ ] Sign-in sheet at the door for the 8:00 setup
