# Prep Checklist

Status: draft

## 6+ weeks out: approvals (these are the long poles)

- [ ] **Approved AI tool confirmed**, with its authorization level (what it may
      hold), confirmed with the command AI or cybersecurity office. Update
      [the data-rules card](../handouts/data-rules-card.md) and the Lab 01 key
- [ ] **Every participant has, or can get, an account** on that tool before the day
- [ ] **Certificate standing confirmed** with the training office: who signs,
      where it's recorded, and whether it carries any credit
      ([certification requirements](../assessment/certification-requirements.md))
- [ ] **Course materials reviewed** under the command's process for training
      content. All examples are fabricated, but confirm none read as real
- [ ] Records manager asked: are AI conversations in this course records, and how should they be kept?
- [ ] Room booked: projector, power at every seat, network access to the approved tool

## 2 weeks out

- [ ] Every lab **run end to end on the approved tool** by someone other than the instructor. Note anything that behaves differently from the answer keys
- [ ] Pre-course email and survey sent ([participants/](../participants/), [survey](../assessment/pre-course-survey.md))
- [ ] Alternate knowledge-check form written, for retakes
- [ ] Capstone submission method set up (email address or form)

## 1 week out

- [ ] Survey read: chase everyone who answered "no access" on question 3
- [ ] Slides finished ([slides/](../slides/))
- [ ] Full dry run against the clock. Capture the outputs, as a fallback if the tool is down
- [ ] Reminder email sent

## Day before

- [ ] Print: participant lab sheets **with the instructor sections removed**, the four cards, knowledge checks, capstone packets, exit tickets, two sign-in sheets, index cards ([handouts/](../handouts/))
- [ ] Wall poster: the data rule
- [ ] Slides on the laptop and on removable media, if allowed

## Morning of (arrive by 7:45)

- [ ] Projector working; the approved tool opens on the instructor machine
- [ ] Agenda on the board
- [ ] Sign-in sheet at the door
