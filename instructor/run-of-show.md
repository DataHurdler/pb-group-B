## If something goes wrong

| Problem | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Running 10+ minutes late | Cut the current lab's stretch task. Then Lab 03 Part C, then Lab 05 Part B. Shorten Module 06 to 20 minutes. Never cut Lab 05 Part C or the close |
| A learner can't log in to ChatGPT | Loaner laptop, or let them follow your projector for that lab and do it on paper. Fix it at the next break |
| A free-tier usage limit stops a learner mid-lab | They switch to the free model ChatGPT offers next, or wait it out and finish the lab on paper. Nothing in the course needs a specific model |
| ChatGPT or Wi-Fi is down for everyone | Run the labs on paper: learners write prompts, and you run volunteers' prompts on your machine or show pre-captured outputs from the dry run |
| Someone pastes real work data | Stop them, gently and at once. Have them delete the chat. Follow the command's incident procedure if it's sensitive. Remind the room of the data rule without naming the person |
| A question on AI policy you can't answer | "That's for `[AI / cybersecurity office POC]`." Write it down and include the answer in the follow-up |
