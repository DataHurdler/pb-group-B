# Changes — Aligning the Course to the Charter

Status: changes from the previous version (commit `514ed7c`), made against
[CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md) and at the request that **learners work
individually rather than in groups**. The structure stays the same: the
same eight modules, in the same order, of the same lengths, in an 8-hour day
with 6 hours of instruction and a 1-hour lunch.

## Where this course deliberately differs from the charter

| Charter says | This course does | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Group work: two groups of learners. Group prompts are run by the instructor on Claude and shown on the projector." | **Individual work.** The instructor projects volunteers' prompts instead of group prompts | Your instruction to gear the course to individual work |
| "About six hours of class, plus lunch and two short breaks" | 6 hours of class, a 1-hour lunch, two 15-minute breaks, **plus 30 minutes of arrival and setup**, making an 8-hour day | Your instruction for an 8-hour workday. The setup slot uses the extra time without adding breaks |
| "Working professionals in mixed roles" | Tailored to USSTRATCOM staff | A narrower audience within the charter's. Flagged here so it's a known choice |

The charter is silent on the certificate of completion, executive writing as
the workplace application, and the no-exam format. Those are design
decisions, kept from earlier versions.

## Unchanged

- Eight modules, in the same order and of the same lengths; 95 teaching and
  265 activity minutes
- All fabricated source texts and answer keys in Labs 02–06
- The four handout cards' content, apart from the data rules card (#6)
- No exam, no graded work, and a certificate for attendance

## Biggest open risk

**Personal laptops with internet access, in a USSTRATCOM training room.** The
charter fixes this tool choice, and a secure facility may not allow it. See
the first item in [instructor/prep-checklist.md](instructor/prep-checklist.md).
