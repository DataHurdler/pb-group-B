# Module 00 — Welcome

**Objective:** None. This module sets up the day.

**Time:** 15 minutes, 8:30–8:45 (10 teaching, 5 activity)

## What it teaches

1. What you'll be able to do by 4:00: the five approved objectives.
2. The data rule: nothing from work, and no confidential data, goes into ChatGPT today.
3. What you'll leave with: a tested template for one recurring task from your own job.

## How it's taught

1. Who I am. Why this course exists: staff time spent turning long inputs into short products for busy leaders.
2. Walk through the [student syllabus](../../handouts/student-syllabus.md): the objectives, the day, no exam, and a certificate of completion for attending.
3. **The data rule**, said out loud and posted on the wall all day: *free ChatGPT is a public tool. Only fabricated course material goes in. No student, personnel, or health records, and nothing from work.* Hand out the [data rules card](../../handouts/data-rules-card.md).
4. Everyone works on their own laptop, individually, all day.

## Activity

1. **5 min, individuals:** on an index card, write **one recurring task from your job** that takes too long, in general terms only ("weekly status roll-up," not its contents). Keep the card: it becomes your template at 1:00.
2. **Whole room:** the instructor asks three or four people to read theirs aloud, and writes them on the board.

## Materials

- Student syllabus, data rules card, index cards, wall poster of the data rule
- Sign-in sheet #1

## Notes for the instructor

- Setup (8:00–8:30) should have every laptop logged in to ChatGPT. Anyone still stuck: work beside them on the projector until the 9:45 break, then fix it.
- **Learners ask:** "Can I use my work task?" Yes, as a *kind* of task. Its real contents never go in. They'll invent a sample input in Module 04.

## If time runs short

Cut the read-aloud. Never cut the data rule.
