# Module 00 — Welcome

Status: draft
Time: 8:00–8:15 (15 min) · Objective(s): none (sets up the day)

## Plan

| Min | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0–3 | Who I am. Why this course exists: staff time spent turning long inputs into short products for busy leaders |
| 3–6 | What you'll be able to do by 4:00, in plain words (the five objectives), using the [student syllabus](../../handouts/student-syllabus.md). No exam: the certificate of completion is for attending the full day |
| 6–9 | **The data rule**, said out loud and posted on the wall for the whole day: *only fabricated, unclassified course material goes into the AI today.* Hand out [handouts/data-rules-card.md](../../handouts/data-rules-card.md) |
| 9–15 | Pairs: name, office, and **one recurring writing task from your week** that takes too long. Collect three or four on the board. Module 04 and the close come back to them |

## Instructor notes

- **Check access first.** Before 8:00, every participant should have logged in
  to the approved tool. Pair anyone without access with someone who has it,
  and fix it at the 9:15 break.
- **Say what the certificate is, and isn't.** It records attendance at this
  course. Its standing beyond that is up to the training office (see
  [course/syllabus.md](../../course/syllabus.md)).
- **Keep the recurring tasks unclassified and generic.** "Weekly status roll-up"
  is fine. Programme or mission details are not.
