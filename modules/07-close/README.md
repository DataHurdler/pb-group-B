# Module 07 — Close: Your 30-Day Plan

**Objective:** LO5. Use prompting strategies that improve efficiency while maintaining output quality.

**Time:** 15 minutes, 3:45–4:00 (5 teaching, 10 activity)

## What it teaches

1. Making AI part of daily work happens in the next 30 days or not at all.
2. At work, the template runs on the command's approved tool, not on free ChatGPT.

## How it's taught

1. Back to the board from the welcome: the recurring tasks the room named. Who now has a tested template for theirs? (3 min)
2. The one change for Monday: same template, the command's approved tool, the same checks. (2 min)

## Activity

1. **7 min, individuals:** write your **30-day plan** on the back of your index card:
   1. The day and time I'll first use my template at work
   2. One more recurring task I'll template next
   3. One task I've decided *not* to use AI for, and why
2. **Whole room:** three or four volunteers read one line aloud.
3. **3 min, individuals:** the exit ticket ([feedback/exit-ticket.md](../../feedback/exit-ticket.md)). The instructor says when certificates of completion arrive, and where the prompt library is. Point to the other courses in the series.

## Materials

- Index cards (from 8:30), exit tickets

## Notes for the instructor

- **Learners ask:** "Can I take my ChatGPT chats home?" They're on their own accounts. Remind them that their template is the thing to keep, and that nothing from work should ever have been in there.

## If time runs short

Never cut this block. If the day runs late, take the minutes from Module 06.

**After the course:** the 30-day follow-up email asks which part of the plan
happened. That answer is the best measure of whether the course worked.
