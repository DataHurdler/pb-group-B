Status: draft

75 minutes. There's no slack, so any addition has to name what it replaces.

**Totals:** 360 minutes, made up of 95 teaching (26%), 225 hands-on (63%),
and 40 assessment (11%).

## Why this order

- **Rules before tools.** The data rule is taught in the first hour, before
  anyone types into the tool unsupervised.
- **The six slots come before executive writing.** Module 03 is the six slots
  applied to the product this audience writes most.
- **Workflow comes after writing.** Module 04 chains the skills from 02 and 03
  into a repeatable process.
- **Checking comes last before assessment.** It's the skill the capstone
  weights most heavily.

## Design notes

- **10:00–12:00 has no break.** Call a 5-minute stretch at the Module 02/03 seam.
- **Module 01 produces something tangible by 9:45.** Each participant has a
  side-by-side comparison of two answers to the same prompt.
- **Assessment is individual. Everything else is in pairs.** The capstone
  still uses the participant's own prompts.
- **If you run late,** take time from the Module 05 stretch task first, then
  shorten the capstone to 25 minutes. Never cut the knowledge check or the
  close: both are needed for certification and feedback.
