Status: draft

**An 8-hour workday, 8:00–4:00: 360 instructional minutes, a 60-minute
lunch, and four 15-minute breaks.** There's no slack, so any addition has to
name what it replaces.

**Totals:** 480 minutes, made up of 360 instruction (95 teaching, 265
hands-on), 60 lunch, and 60 breaks.

There's no exam or graded work. Participants who attend the whole day receive
a certificate of completion.

## Why this order

- **Rules before tools.** The data rule is taught in the first hour, before
  anyone types into the tool unsupervised.
- **The six slots come before executive writing.** Module 03 is the six slots
  applied to the product this audience writes most.
- **Workflow comes after writing.** Module 04 chains the skills from 02 and 03
  into a repeatable process.
- **Checking comes right before the final exercise.** Module 06 is where it
  matters most.

## Design notes

- **A break follows every module before lunch and after it.** No block runs
  longer than 60 minutes without a pause.
- **Module 01 produces something tangible by 9:15.** Each participant has a
  side-by-side comparison of two answers to the same prompt.
- **If you run late,** take time from the stretch tasks first, then shorten
  Module 06 to 20 minutes. Never cut the close.
