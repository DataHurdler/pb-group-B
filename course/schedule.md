# Schedule — The Day

Status: draft

**An 8-hour workday, 8:00–4:00:** a 30-minute arrival and setup, 360
instructional minutes, a 60-minute lunch, and **two** 15-minute breaks (as the
charter requires). Everyone works **individually**. Any addition has to name
what it replaces.

**Totals:** 480 minutes, made up of 360 instruction (95 teaching, 265
individual activity), 30 setup, 60 lunch, and 30 breaks.

There's no exam or graded work. Participants who attend the whole day receive
a certificate of completion.

## Why this order

- **Rules before tools.** The data rule is taught in the first hour, before
  anyone types into ChatGPT unsupervised.
- **The six slots come before executive writing.** Module 03 is the six slots
  applied to the product this audience writes most.
- **Workflow comes before testing.** Module 04 produces each learner's own
  template, and Module 05 tests and refines it. That's the charter's
  take-home.
- **The decision memo comes last.** It applies everything, and starts from a
  library template, which is efficiency in practice.

## Design notes

- **Setup is outside the 360 minutes.** It's for logging in on personal
  laptops, which fails often enough to deserve its own slot.
- **10:00–12:00 and 2:15–4:00 have no break.** Call a 2-minute stretch at
  11:00 and at 3:15.
- **Debriefs are whole-room.** The instructor projects one or two volunteers'
  prompts. There is no group work.
- **If you run late,** take time from the stretch tasks first, then shorten
  Module 06 to 20 minutes. Never cut Lab 05 Part C (testing your own template)
  or the close.
