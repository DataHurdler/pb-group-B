# Setup Instructions

Status: draft

Finish this **before the day**. It takes about 10 minutes. There's nothing to
install. Arrival and setup also run from 8:00 to 8:30 on the day, for anyone
who gets stuck.

1. **Bring your own laptop** with a current web browser.
   - Check: it charges, and it can join a Wi-Fi network.
2. **Sign up for, or log in to, a free ChatGPT account** at chatgpt.com.
   Use a personal account. If you're unsure whether that's allowed, check
   your command's guidance first. No paid plan is needed, and none is used.
   - Check: you can log in.
3. **Run a test prompt.** Paste exactly:
   > In three bullets, explain what a BLUF email is.
   - Check: you get an answer back.
4. **Find "new chat"**, and the setting for memory (or a temporary chat), if
   your account has one. You'll start many new chats during the day.
   - Check: you can open a new, empty chat.

**If any step fails,** email `[ADDRESS]` by `[DATE – 5 days]`, or come at 8:00
on the day.

**The one rule for the day:** free ChatGPT is a public tool. Only the
fabricated course material goes into it. Leave your real work out of it.
