# Capstone Rubric — The Decision Memo

Pass = **"Meets" or higher on every row.** Rows 1 and 2 are gates: "Does not
meet" on either is a fail, whatever the other rows score.

| # | Criterion | Does not meet | Meets | Exceeds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Data handling** (LO1) *gate* | Pasted anything other than the packet, or used an unapproved tool | Only packet content, on the approved tool | — |
| 2 | **Accuracy** (LO5) *gate* | Any number, date, or policy not in the packet; or 92% presented as measured | Every fact traces to the packet; the estimate is labeled | Verification notes link each number to its packet line |
| 3 | **BLUF** (LO3) | Decision or deadline missing from the first two sentences | Decision needed and 15 Nov deadline both in the first two sentences | The reader could act on the first sentence alone |
| 4 | **Options and recommendation** (LO3) | Options missing, or no recommendation | All three options with hours, cost, and completion; a recommendation with a reason | Uses the director's 90% criterion explicitly; names the risk in the recommended option |
| 5 | **Executive format** (LO3) | Over one page, or a wall of text | One page, short paragraphs or bullets, numbers not adjectives | Could go up the chain with no edits |
| 6 | **Prompt quality** (LO2) | One vague prompt | Prompts fill at least Task, Context (with "use only"), Audience, and Format | All six slots, or steps chained with a check between them |
| 7 | **Reusable template** (LO4) | Missing, or specific to this packet | Specifics replaced with `[SLOTS]`; would work on a different packet | A colleague could use it cold, with the data rule built in |

## Scoring notes

- **Score from the submission, not from memory of the room.**
- **Either A or C can be the right recommendation.** Score the reasoning and
  the accuracy, not the choice. B fails row 4 only if it's recommended without
  addressing the 90% criterion.
- **Record row-level scores**, not just pass or fail. Resubmissions need
  feedback on the failing rows.
