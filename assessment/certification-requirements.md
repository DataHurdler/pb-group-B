# Certification Requirements

Status: draft. Have the training office confirm the certificate's standing before the course is advertised.

A participant receives the **Certificate of Completion: AI Prompt Engineering
for Government Staff Work** when they meet **all three** requirements:

| # | Requirement | Evidence | Assesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Attend every module** (00–07). Up to 15 minutes' total absence is allowed with instructor approval | Sign-in sheet at 8:30 and 12:45 | — |
| 2 | **Knowledge check: 8 or more out of 10**, individual, closed-book, at 2:50 | [knowledge-check.md](knowledge-check.md) | LO1–LO5 |
| 3 | **Capstone: "Meets" or higher on every rubric row**, individual, at 3:00 | [capstone-rubric.md](capstone-rubric.md) | LO1–LO5 |

## If a participant doesn't pass

- **Knowledge check below 8:** one retake of an alternate form, by email,
  within 14 days.
- **Capstone below "Meets" on any row:** one resubmission within 14 days, with
  written feedback on the failing rows.
- **Missed a module:** attend that module at a later offering.

## Records

- Scores and certificate dates go to `[TRAINING RECORD SYSTEM / OFFICE]`.
- Capstone submissions are kept for `[RETENTION PERIOD, per records manager]`.
- Certificates are issued within 2 business days of passing, using
  [certificate-template.md](certificate-template.md).

## What the certificate is

It records that the participant completed this course and showed the five
objectives on the day. It is **not** an authorization to use any AI tool:
that comes from the command's AI-use policy and the participant's account
access. Any continuing-education hours or formal credential status are set
by `[TRAINING OFFICE]`.
