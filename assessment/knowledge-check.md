# Knowledge Check

*Individual, closed-book, 10 minutes. Circle one answer for each question.
Passing is 8 out of 10.*

Name: ______________________ Office: ______________

**1. (LO1)** You paste the same prompt into two new conversations and get two
different answers. Why?

- a) The AI predicts likely text, so its output varies from run to run
- b) One conversation had a bug
- c) The AI looked up different websites each time
- d) The second answer is always more accurate

**2. (LO1)** You ask the AI for your directorate's report deadline and it gives
a specific date. Most likely:

- a) It found the date in a command database
- b) It's correct, because it sounds confident
- c) It produced a plausible date, since it has no way to know the real one
- d) It remembered the date from an earlier user

**3. (LO1)** On a tool authorized for unclassified information only, which may
you paste?

- a) A spreadsheet of names and home addresses
- b) A public press release
- c) A draft marked CUI
- d) A colleague's appraisal draft with the name removed

**4. (LO2)** Your output is the right length but full of invented details about
your office. Which slot is most likely empty?

- a) Format
- b) Example
- c) Audience
- d) Context

**5. (LO2)** Which instruction best reduces invented facts?

- a) "Act as a world-class expert."
- b) "Be accurate."
- c) "Write in a professional tone."
- d) "Use only the text I provided. If it isn't there, say 'not in source'."

**6. (LO3)** In a BLUF email to a senior leader, the first sentence should:

- a) Explain the background of the issue
- b) State the decision or action needed, and the deadline
- c) Thank the reader for their time
- d) List the people who coordinated

**7. (LO3)** The AI's draft says "significant impact on personnel." The
executive-writing fix is to:

- a) Replace it with the actual number, such as "140 people for 2 weeks"
- b) Make it bold
- c) Move it to the last paragraph
- d) Say "very significant" instead

**8. (LO4)** Why split "extract actions from notes" and "write the follow-up
email" into two prompts?

- a) The AI can only handle one sentence at a time
- b) You can check the extraction before errors are repeated in the email
- c) It uses fewer words
- d) Two prompts are required by policy

**9. (LO4)** A prompt template is finished when:

- a) A colleague can use it without asking you anything
- b) It's longer than one page
- c) It worked once for you
- d) It contains the words "you are an expert"

**10. (LO5)** An AI summary says "Per DoD policy, offices below 90% must report
monthly." The source doesn't say this. This is:

- a) Acceptable, because it's probably true
- b) A formatting issue
- c) An invented authority. Remove it, or find and cite the actual policy
- d) Fine if the rest of the summary is accurate
