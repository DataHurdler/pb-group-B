# Lab 06 — Capstone: The Decision Memo

Status: draft
Module: 06 · Time: 30 min · Objective(s): all · Work: **individual, assessed**

## Your task

The director has to decide how to deliver next year's records-management
refresher. Using the packet below and your own prompts on the approved tool,
produce a **one-page decision memo** that the director can act on in under a
minute.

The memo must include:

- a **BLUF** stating the decision needed and the deadline
- the **options**, each with its cost and expected completion
- your **recommendation**, with a one-line reason
- **only facts from the packet**, with estimates labeled as estimates

## Submit (by 3:28)

1. **Every prompt** you used, in order
2. **The final memo**
3. **Verification notes:** each number in your memo, with where in the packet
   it came from
4. **One reusable template** that would produce this kind of memo from a
   different packet, using `[SLOTS]`

Scoring: [assessment/capstone-rubric.md](../assessment/capstone-rubric.md).

## Packet (fabricated for training, unclassified)

> **From:** Training Office lead
> **To:** Director's front office
> **Subject:** RE: RE: Records refresher — FY27 delivery
>
> Following up on the director's question. Three ways to run it for the
> 270 personnel who need it:
>
> **Option A: in-person sessions.** Six 2-hour sessions of 45 people. Last
> year's completion rate with this format was 97%. No outside cost, since our
> own instructors teach it, but it uses 540 staff-hours.
>
> **Option B: online self-paced module (45 min).** A pilot in FY25 reached
> 81% completion by the deadline. About 203 staff-hours. License: $3,200 per
> year.
>
> **Option C: hybrid.** The online module plus one 30-minute live Q&A. We
> *estimate* 92% completion, but we have no data on this format yet. About
> 338 staff-hours if everyone attends the Q&A. License: $3,200 per year.
>
> We need a decision by 15 Nov to schedule sessions for January.
>
> > **Front office note:** The director wants to cut staff-hours where
> > possible but won't accept completion below 90%.

| Option | Staff-hours | Cost | Completion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — In person | 540 | $0 | 97% (last year) |
| B — Online | ~203 | $3,200/yr | 81% (FY25 pilot) |
| C — Hybrid | ~338 | $3,200/yr | 92% (**estimate, no data**) |

## Instructor: what a strong memo does

- **BLUF:** decision needed by 15 Nov on the refresher format. The
  recommendation is in the first two sentences.
- **Eliminates B** using the director's own criterion: 81% is below 90%.
- **Recommends A or C, either with a reason.** C saves about 200 staff-hours
  but its 92% is unproven. A is proven at 97% but costs 540 hours. A strong
  memo labels C's figure as an estimate. The best memos suggest a fallback,
  such as C with a check partway through and A sessions in reserve. That
  counts only if it's framed as the writer's suggestion, not a packet fact.
- **Every number traces to the packet.** The 270-person total and the
  staff-hour figures appear as given, not recalculated differently.

**Automatic fail on the accuracy row:** presenting 92% as measured, or citing
any number, policy, or deadline that isn't in the packet.
