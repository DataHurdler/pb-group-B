## What this course is

A hands-on day on using your command's approved AI tool for everyday staff
work. You'll learn to write prompts that work the first time, turn long, messy
inputs into short products a senior leader can act on in under a minute, and
build AI into the recurring tasks of your week, safely. You'll spend most of
the day working in pairs, not listening. **There is no exam and no graded
work.**

## By 4:00 you'll be able to

1. **Explain how the AI produces its answers,** and follow the data rules for
   what may and may not go into it.
2. **Write a structured prompt** using the six slots (Task, Context, Audience,
   Format, Constraints, Example), and fix a weak one.
3. **Produce executive-ready products** with AI, such as a BLUF email, a
   one-page information paper, or a decision memo, and edit the draft until
   you'd sign it.
4. **Turn a recurring task into a reusable template** you can run every week.
5. **Check AI output against its source,** catch invented facts, and know when
   not to use AI.

## The one rule for today

> **Only the fabricated, unclassified course material goes into the AI.**
> Don't paste real work documents, names, or anything sensitive, even to
> "try it out." Every exercise text is invented for training.

At work afterwards, use only the AI tool your command has approved, and only
for what it's authorized to hold. Your **data rules card** explains this.

## How the day works

- **You'll work in pairs** on the approved tool. Talk through your prompts.
  Explaining why you wrote something is half the learning.
- **Start a new conversation for each test.** Earlier messages change later
  answers.
- **The AI drafts, and you sign.** Check every number, date, and name before
  anything goes up the chain.
- **Ask questions any time.** Policy questions the instructor can't answer go
  to `[AI / CYBERSECURITY OFFICE POC]`, and the answers come in the follow-up
  email.
