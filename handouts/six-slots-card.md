# The Six Slots — A Prompt Checklist

*When the output disappoints, ask which slot is empty.*

| Slot | Ask yourself | Example line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Task** | What exactly should it produce? | "Draft a BLUF email…" |
| **Context** | What does it need that it can't know? | "Here are the facts: [PASTE]. Use only these." |
| **Audience** | Who reads it, and what do they need? | "…for the Chief of Staff, who has to decide on relocation." |
| **Format** | Length, structure, order? | "≤120 words, subject line, 3 bullets max, decision in the first sentence." |
| **Constraints** | What must it not do? | "Don't add facts, dates, or policies that aren't in the source." |
| **Example** | What does good look like? | "Match the tone and length of this example: [PASTE]." |

## Four habits

1. **Paste the source, and say "use only this."**
2. **One step per prompt.** Extract, then organize, then draft, then check.
3. **Ask it to ask you:** "Before you write, ask me any questions you need answered."
4. **New conversation, new test.** Earlier turns change later answers.
