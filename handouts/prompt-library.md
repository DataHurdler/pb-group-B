# Prompt Library — Starter Templates for Daily Work

*Copy a template, fill every `[SLOT]`, and check the output before using it.
Data rule first: paste only what your approved tool is authorized to hold.*

## 1. BLUF email from notes
> Turn these notes into a BLUF email to `[READER]`, ≤`[N]` words. The first
> sentence states `[THE ASK / CONCLUSION]` and `[DEADLINE]`. Use only these
> notes, and don't add facts: `[PASTE NOTES]`

## 2. Meeting notes → action table
> From these meeting notes, make a table with the columns Action | Owner |
> Due | Status. Use only the notes. Where the owner or date is unclear, write
> UNASSIGNED or TBD. Don't guess. Notes: `[PASTE NOTES]`

## 3. Follow-up email from an action table
> Using only this action table, draft a follow-up email to `[ATTENDEES]`,
> ≤150 words. BLUF: the unassigned items and the next meeting
> (`[DATE/TIME]`). Then the table. Table: `[PASTE TABLE]`

## 4. Weekly status entry
> Write a weekly status entry, ≤3 lines, for `[PROJECT / LINE OF EFFORT]`,
> with the headings Done, Next, and Issues needing leadership attention. Use
> only these updates: `[PASTE UPDATES]`

## 5. Shorten for a senior reader
> Cut this to ≤`[N]` words for `[READER]`, who needs to `[DECIDE / KNOW]`
> `[WHAT]`. Keep every number, date, and caveat. Remove background they
> already know: `[PASTE TEXT]`

## 6. Information paper
> One-page information paper for `[READER]` on `[TOPIC]`. Sections: Purpose,
> Bottom line, Key facts (≤5 bullets), and Way ahead. Use only this source,
> and mark anything uncertain `[VERIFY]`: `[PASTE SOURCE]`

## 7. Decision memo
> Decision memo for `[READER]`. BLUF: the decision needed by `[DATE]`. Give
> each option with its cost, effect, and pros and cons, then a recommendation
> with a reason. Label estimates. Use only: `[PASTE FACTS]`

## 8. Check a draft against its source
> Below are a source and a draft. List every claim in the draft. For each,
> quote the supporting source sentence or write NOT IN SOURCE. Then list
> anything important in the source that the draft left out.
> SOURCE: `[PASTE]` DRAFT: `[PASTE]`
