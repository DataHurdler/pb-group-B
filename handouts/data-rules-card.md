# Data Rules Card — What Goes Into the AI

*Keep this on your desk. If you're unsure, the answer is "check first."*

## Today, in class: free ChatGPT on your own laptop

Free ChatGPT is a **public tool, not an approved government system.**

- ✅ **Only** this course's fabricated material, public documents, and your own general writing
- ⛔ **Nothing from work**, even with the names removed
- ⛔ **No confidential data, ever:** no student, personnel, or health records

## Three rules that don't change

1. **Removing names doesn't make it releasable.** You're responsible for
   what the information *is*, not just how it's marked.
2. **The writer owns the output.** Check every fact before it goes up the
   chain.
3. **AI conversations at work may be federal records.** Ask your records
   manager how to keep them.
