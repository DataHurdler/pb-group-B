# Data Rules Card — What Goes Into the AI

*Keep this on your desk. If you're unsure, the answer is "check first."*

**Today, in class:** only the fabricated, unclassified course material goes in.

**At work:** use only the AI tool your command has approved for your account,
within what it is authorized to hold. Approved tool: `[TOOL NAME]`.
Authorized for: `[AUTHORIZATION LEVEL, e.g., unclassified / CUI. Confirm with
the command AI or cybersecurity office before printing]`.

| ✅ OK | ⚠️ CHECK FIRST | ⛔ NEVER |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public documents: press releases, published policy | Unmarked internal material that may still be sensitive | **Anything classified**, at any level, on an unclassified tool |
| Your own general writing and notes with no sensitive content | Vendor quotes, proposals, or anything procurement-related | **PII**: names with home addresses, SSNs, medical information |
| Fabricated or training material | Anything about a programme, schedule, or capability | **Personnel actions**: evaluations, discipline, selection |
| Formatting, rewording, and structure requests with no sensitive content | CUI, unless your tool is authorized for it | Anything the tool isn't authorized to hold |

**Three rules that don't change:**

1. **Removing names doesn't make it releasable.** You're responsible for
   what the information *is*, not just how it's marked.
2. **The writer owns the output.** Check every fact before it goes up the
   chain.
3. **AI conversations may be federal records.** Ask your records manager how
   to keep them.
