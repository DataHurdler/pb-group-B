# Executive Writing with AI

*Your reader has 45 seconds. AI does the restructuring. You decide what
matters and sign it.*

## The four rules

1. **BLUF.** The first sentence is the decision, the ask, or the conclusion.
2. **The ask and the date.** "Decision needed by 22 Aug" rather than "for awareness."
3. **Numbers, not adjectives.** "140 people, 2 weeks" rather than "significant impact."
4. **One page.** Short paragraphs, active voice. Detail goes in an attachment.

## Three products, three prompts

**BLUF email**
> Rewrite the facts below as a BLUF email to `[READER]`, ≤`[N]` words, with a
> subject line that starts with `[DECISION NEEDED / INFO / ACTION]`. The first
> sentence states `[THE ASK]` and `[THE DEADLINE]`. Use only these facts:
> `[PASTE]`

**One-page information paper**
> Write a one-page information paper for `[READER]` on `[TOPIC]`. Sections:
> Purpose (1 sentence), Bottom line (2 sentences), Key facts (≤5 bullets,
> numbers included), Way ahead. Use only this source: `[PASTE]`. Mark anything
> uncertain as `[VERIFY]`.

**Decision memo**
> Write a decision memo for `[READER]`. BLUF: the decision needed and the
> deadline. Then each option with its cost, effect, and one-line pros and
> cons, followed by a recommendation with a one-line reason. Label estimates as
> estimates. Use only these facts: `[PASTE]`

## Before you send: the sign-off check

- [ ] Could the reader act on the first two sentences alone?
- [ ] Is the deadline in the first sentence?
- [ ] Have I checked every number, date, and name against the source by hand?
- [ ] Is anything added that isn't in the source?
- [ ] Is every estimate labeled as an estimate, and every caveat kept?
- [ ] Does it follow the command's correspondence format?
